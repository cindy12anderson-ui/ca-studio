Anything else? Ask me in your demo request.

How much does a website cost?

Your website is a one-time fixed price, quoted after your free demo. You know exactly what you’re paying before anything starts, with no hourly surprises.

What do I need to get started?

Just your business name and your Instagram or current website. I’ll design a working demo from there, and we refine the words and photos together.

Can you connect my booking system?

Yes. I connect the platform you already use, like Fresha or Square, so clients can book in a tap. Google reviews and local search come built in.

Will it look good on phones?

Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops, because that’s where most of your clients will find you.

Can I keep updating my site after launch?

Of course! With a monthly care plan, new prices, services, photos and seasonal offers are handled for you, so your site never goes out of date.
